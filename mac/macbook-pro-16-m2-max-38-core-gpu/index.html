MacBook Pro 16″ M2 Max (38-core GPU): gaming performance

The MacBook Pro 16″ M2 Max (38-core GPU) (2023) is a strong gaming Mac. In native games it runs Cyberpunk 2077 at about 73 FPS and Baldur's Gate 3 at about 68 FPS at 1080p High, and League of Legends at about 344 FPS. Windows games through CrossOver, like Counter-Strike 2, reach about 168 FPS. Its 38-core GPU performs roughly like a desktop RTX 5050 in native games. With 32 GB of unified memory, about 20.8 GB is available for graphics.

MacBook Pro 16″ M2 Max (38-core GPU): quick answers

Is the MacBook Pro 16″ M2 Max (38-core GPU) good for gaming?

The MacBook Pro 16″ M2 Max (38-core GPU) (2023) is a strong gaming Mac. Expect about 73 FPS in Cyberpunk 2077 and 344 FPS in League of Legends at 1080p High.

What PC is the MacBook Pro 16″ M2 Max (38-core GPU) equal to for gaming?

In native Mac games its graphics perform roughly like a RTX 5050, with CPU performance close to a Core i5-10600K. Windows games through CrossOver run about a third slower.

How much memory does the MacBook Pro 16″ M2 Max (38-core GPU) need for gaming?

32 GB gives about 20.8 GB to graphics, which is enough for 1080p–1440p High. Configurations up to 96 GB help at 4K.
