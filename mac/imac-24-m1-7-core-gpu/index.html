iMac 24″ M1 (7-core GPU): gaming performance

The iMac 24″ M1 (7-core GPU) (2021) is best suited to light games and cloud gaming. In native games it runs Cyberpunk 2077 at about 17 FPS and Baldur's Gate 3 at about 21 FPS at 1080p High, and League of Legends at about 106 FPS. Windows games through CrossOver, like Counter-Strike 2, reach about 39 FPS. Its 7-core GPU performs roughly like a desktop RX 6400 in native games. With 16 GB of unified memory, about 10.4 GB is available for graphics.

iMac 24″ M1 (7-core GPU): quick answers

Is the iMac 24″ M1 (7-core GPU) good for gaming?

The iMac 24″ M1 (7-core GPU) (2021) is best suited to light games and cloud gaming. Expect about 17 FPS in Cyberpunk 2077 and 106 FPS in League of Legends at 1080p High.

What PC is the iMac 24″ M1 (7-core GPU) equal to for gaming?

In native Mac games its graphics perform roughly like a RX 6400, with CPU performance close to a Ryzen 5 4600G. Windows games through CrossOver run about a third slower.

How much memory does the iMac 24″ M1 (7-core GPU) need for gaming?

Avoid the 8 GB version for modern games. 16 GB gives about 10.4 GB to graphics, which is enough for 1080p–1440p High.
