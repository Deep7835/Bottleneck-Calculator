Ryzen 5 7600 vs Ryzen 5 7500F: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

You won't feel a difference between the Ryzen 5 7600 and Ryzen 5 7500F in most games. With an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, our model has them at 136 vs 134 FPS. In esports games like CS2 it's 354 vs 348 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 75 vs 74 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 95 vs 94 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 100 and 100 FPS, effectively the same. Both use AM5, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap after a BIOS update. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. Both have 6 cores and 12 threads. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 5080 at 1440p.

Ryzen 5 7600 vs Ryzen 5 7500F: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 5 7600 better than the Ryzen 5 7500F for gaming?

They're within 1% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 5 7600 and Ryzen 5 7500F get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 5 7600 averages about 136 FPS at 1080p and 135 at 1440p in AAA games, and 354 FPS in esports titles. The Ryzen 5 7500F averages 134, 133 and 348 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 5 7600 or Ryzen 5 7500F?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 5 7600 keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080 and the Ryzen 5 7500F keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
