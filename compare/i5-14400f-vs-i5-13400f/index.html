Core i5-14400F vs Core i5-13400F: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

You won't feel a difference between the Core i5-14400F and Core i5-13400F in most games. With an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, our model has them at 128 vs 126 FPS. In esports games like CS2 it's 333 vs 328 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 70 vs 69 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 90 vs 88 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 99 and 99 FPS, effectively the same. Both use LGA1700, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. Both have 10 cores and 16 threads. Under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the Core i5-14400F can drive up to the RTX 5080 and the Core i5-13400F up to the RX 7900 XTX.

Core i5-14400F vs Core i5-13400F: quick answers

Is the Core i5-14400F better than the Core i5-13400F for gaming?

They're within 2% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Core i5-14400F and Core i5-13400F get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Core i5-14400F averages about 128 FPS at 1080p and 128 at 1440p in AAA games, and 333 FPS in esports titles. The Core i5-13400F averages 126, 126 and 328 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Core i5-14400F or Core i5-13400F?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Core i5-14400F keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080 and the Core i5-13400F keeps up with cards up to the RX 7900 XTX. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
