Ryzen 7 9850X3D vs Ryzen 7 9800X3D: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

You won't feel a difference between the Ryzen 7 9850X3D and Ryzen 7 9800X3D in most games. With an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, our model has them at 204 vs 199 FPS. In esports games like CS2 it's 535 vs 519 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 113 vs 110 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 151 vs 147 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 101 FPS, effectively the same. Both use AM5, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap after a BIOS update. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. Both have 8 cores and 16 threads. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 5090 at 1440p.

Ryzen 7 9850X3D vs Ryzen 7 9800X3D: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 7 9850X3D better than the Ryzen 7 9800X3D for gaming?

They're within 3% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 7 9850X3D and Ryzen 7 9800X3D get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 7 9850X3D averages about 204 FPS at 1080p and 186 at 1440p in AAA games, and 535 FPS in esports titles. The Ryzen 7 9800X3D averages 199, 183 and 519 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 7 9850X3D or Ryzen 7 9800X3D?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 7 9850X3D keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5090 and the Ryzen 7 9800X3D keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5090. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
