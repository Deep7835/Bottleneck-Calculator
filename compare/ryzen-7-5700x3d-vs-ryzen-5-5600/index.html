Ryzen 7 5700X3D vs Ryzen 5 5600: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The Ryzen 7 5700X3D is in a different league: 25% faster than the Ryzen 5 5600 at 1080p with an RTX 5090, or 132 vs 106 FPS in AAA games at High settings. The Ryzen 7 5700X3D's 3D V-Cache is the reason: the extra L3 cache keeps more game data close to the cores. The gap holds at 1440p (24%) because even there the CPU, not the RTX 5090, sets the frame rate. In esports games like CS2 it's 343 vs 276 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 73 vs 58 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 98 vs 74 FPS, helped by the Ryzen 7 5700X3D's large cache. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 100 and 95 FPS, 5% apart. Both use AM4, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap after a BIOS update. The Ryzen 7 5700X3D has more threads (16 vs 12), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Ryzen 7 5700X3D if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 25% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card. The Ryzen 5 5600 makes sense if it costs at least 24% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Ryzen 5 5600? Going to the Ryzen 7 5700X3D gains 25% at 1080p, which is a worthwhile upgrade. It drops into the same motherboard.

Buy the Ryzen 7 5700X3D if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 25% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 98 vs 74 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 73 vs 58 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 8 cores / 16 threads vs 6 / 12.
  • you plan a faster graphics card: it keeps up with GPUs up to the RTX 5080 at 1440p.

Buy the Ryzen 5 5600 if…

  • it costs at least 25% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.
  • you pair it with a mid-range card: with an RTX 5070 at 1440p the two are only 5% apart.

Ryzen 7 5700X3D vs Ryzen 5 5600: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 7 5700X3D better than the Ryzen 5 5600 for gaming?

Yes. The Ryzen 7 5700X3D is about 25% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 24% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 7 5700X3D and Ryzen 5 5600 get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 7 5700X3D averages about 132 FPS at 1080p and 131 at 1440p in AAA games, and 343 FPS in esports titles. The Ryzen 5 5600 averages 106, 106 and 276 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 7 5700X3D or Ryzen 5 5600?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 7 5700X3D keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080 and the Ryzen 5 5600 keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4070 Ti Super. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
