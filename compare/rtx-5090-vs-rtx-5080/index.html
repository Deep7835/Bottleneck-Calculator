RTX 5090 vs RTX 5080: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 5090 sits in a higher performance class: 183 FPS at 1440p High against 140 for the RTX 5080 with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and a 41% lead at 4K. At 1080p the lead shrinks to 8%, because even a 9800X3D can't feed the RTX 5090 fast enough there, so it earns its keep at 1440p and 4K. Ray tracing widens the gap to 40% (115 vs 82 FPS at 1440p), as ray tracing needs extra VRAM. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 519 and 510 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K High the RTX 5090 averages 120 FPS and the RTX 5080 86, both playable without upscaling. The RTX 5090 also has more memory (32 GB vs 16 GB), though both have enough for 4K High today. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RTX 5090 needs a Ryzen 7 9800X3D and the RTX 5080 a Ryzen 7 5700X3D.

RTX 5090 vs RTX 5080: quick answers

Is the RTX 5090 faster than the RTX 5080?

Yes. The RTX 5090 is about 41% faster at 4K and 30% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 5090 gets about 115 FPS and the RTX 5080 82 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 5090 and RTX 5080 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 5090 averages about 199 FPS at 1080p, 183 at 1440p and 120 at 4K. The RTX 5080 averages 184, 140 and 86 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 5090 or RTX 5080?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 5090 needs at least a Ryzen 7 9800X3D and the RTX 5080 needs at least a Ryzen 7 5700X3D. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 5090 and the RTX 5080.
