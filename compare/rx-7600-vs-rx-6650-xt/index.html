RX 7600 vs RX 6650 XT: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

Expect a small but real lead for the RX 6650 XT: 46 FPS at 1440p High against 43 for the RX 7600 with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, a 7% gap that is about the same at 4K. Newer isn't faster here: the RX 6650 XT came out 1 year before the RX 7600 and still leads in raster performance. The order holds at 1080p (7% apart), so the RX 6650 XT is the quicker card at every resolution. Turn on ray tracing and the order flips: the RX 7600 leads 16 to 14 FPS at 1440p, because the RX 6650 XT's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 213 and 228 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor. At 4K the RX 7600 manages 24 FPS native, and FSR Quality lifts that to about 35. Both carry 8 GB of VRAM, which is already tight at 1440p High in new games. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

Buy the RX 6650 XT if you want the faster card: it's 7% ahead at 4K. RX 7600 vs RX 6650 XT: quick answers

Is the RX 7600 faster than the RX 6650 XT?

No. The RX 6650 XT is about 7% faster at 4K and 7% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 7600 gets about 16 FPS and the RX 6650 XT 14 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 7600 and RX 6650 XT get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 7600 averages about 67 FPS at 1080p, 43 at 1440p and 24 at 4K. The RX 6650 XT averages 71, 46 and 26 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 7600 or RX 6650 XT?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 7600 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RX 6650 XT needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RX 6650 XT and the RX 7600.
