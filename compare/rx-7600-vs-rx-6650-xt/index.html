RX 7600 vs RX 6650 XT: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

There is almost nothing between the RX 7600 and RX 6650 XT. Paired with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, our model puts them at 48 vs 46 FPS at 1440p High, and within 3% of each other at 4K. At 1080p they're 3% apart too. Ray tracing widens the gap to 22% (16 vs 14 FPS at 1440p), as the RX 6650 XT's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 235 and 228 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor. At 4K the RX 6650 XT manages 26 FPS native, and FSR Quality lifts that to about 37. Both carry 8 GB of VRAM, which is already tight at 1440p High in new games. With frame rates this close, price and the features you value should decide, not benchmarks. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

Which should you buy?

On raster performance this is a coin toss, so buy whichever is cheaper where you shop. If you play with ray tracing, the RX 7600 is the safer pick (22% faster with RT at 1440p).

Buy the RX 7600 if…

  • you want the most FPS: it's 3% faster at 4K and 3% at 1440p.
  • you play with ray tracing: 16 vs 14 FPS at 1440p.
  • you want the newer card (2023) with the longer driver and feature support ahead of it.

Buy the RX 6650 XT if…

  • it costs at least 3% less than the RX 7600, which makes it the better value per frame.
  • you play at 1080p, where the gap is only 3% and upscaling hides it further.

RX 7600 vs RX 6650 XT: quick answers

Is the RX 7600 faster than the RX 6650 XT?

They're within 3% of each other at 4K and 3% at 1440p, so in practice they perform the same. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 7600 gets about 16 FPS and the RX 6650 XT 14 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 7600 and RX 6650 XT get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 7600 averages about 74 FPS at 1080p, 48 at 1440p and 26 at 4K. The RX 6650 XT averages 71, 46 and 26 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 7600 or RX 6650 XT?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 7600 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RX 6650 XT needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine.
