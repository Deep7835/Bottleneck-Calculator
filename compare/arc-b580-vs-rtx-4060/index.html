Arc B580 vs RTX 4060: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at 1440p High, the Arc B580 averages 55 FPS in AAA games to the RTX 4060's 49. That 11% lead grows to 21% at 4K. It's a cross-brand matchup: Intel's 2024 card against NVIDIA's 2023 card. The order holds at 1080p (3% apart), so the Arc B580 is the quicker card at every resolution. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 29 and 26 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 250 and 243 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 4060 manages 27 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 39. The Arc B580 also has more memory (12 GB vs 8 GB), and 8 GB is already tight at 1440p High in new games. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

Arc B580 vs RTX 4060: quick answers

Is the Arc B580 faster than the RTX 4060?

Yes. The Arc B580 is about 21% faster at 4K and 11% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the Arc B580 gets about 29 FPS and the RTX 4060 26 FPS.

How many FPS do the Arc B580 and RTX 4060 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the Arc B580 averages about 78 FPS at 1080p, 55 at 1440p and 33 at 4K. The RTX 4060 averages 76, 49 and 27 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the Arc B580 or RTX 4060?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Arc B580 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RTX 4060 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the Arc B580 and the RTX 4060.
