Ryzen 5 5600 vs Ryzen 5 5500: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

Paired with an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, the Ryzen 5 5600 pushes 106 FPS in AAA games against 90 for the Ryzen 5 5500, a clear 18% advantage. The gap holds at 1440p (18%) because even there the CPU, not the RTX 5090, sets the frame rate. In esports games like CS2 it's 276 vs 234 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 58 vs 49 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 74 vs 63 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 95 and 86 FPS, 10% apart. Both use AM4, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap after a BIOS update. Both have 6 cores and 12 threads. Under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the Ryzen 5 5600 can drive up to the RTX 4070 Ti Super and the Ryzen 5 5500 up to the RTX 4070 Super.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Ryzen 5 5600 if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 18% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card. The Ryzen 5 5500 makes sense if it costs at least 18% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Ryzen 5 5500? Going to the Ryzen 5 5600 gains 18% at 1080p, which is a modest upgrade, worth it mainly if you can sell the Ryzen 5 5500. It drops into the same motherboard.

Buy the Ryzen 5 5600 if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 18% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 74 vs 63 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 58 vs 49 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you plan a faster graphics card: it keeps up with GPUs up to the RTX 4070 Ti Super at 1440p.

Buy the Ryzen 5 5500 if…

  • it costs at least 18% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.
  • it's the cheaper of the two where you shop: gaming performance is close enough that price decides.

Ryzen 5 5600 vs Ryzen 5 5500: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 5 5600 better than the Ryzen 5 5500 for gaming?

Yes. The Ryzen 5 5600 is about 18% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 18% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 5 5600 and Ryzen 5 5500 get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 5 5600 averages about 106 FPS at 1080p and 106 at 1440p in AAA games, and 276 FPS in esports titles. The Ryzen 5 5500 averages 90, 90 and 234 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 5 5600 or Ryzen 5 5500?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 5 5600 keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4070 Ti Super and the Ryzen 5 5500 keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4070 Super. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
