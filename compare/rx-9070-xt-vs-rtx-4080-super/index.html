RX 9070 XT vs RTX 4080 Super: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

Expect a small but real lead for the RTX 4080 Super: 128 FPS at 1440p High against 122 for the RX 9070 XT with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, a 5% gap that is about the same at 4K. Newer isn't faster here: the RTX 4080 Super came out 1 year before the RX 9070 XT and still leads in raster performance. The order holds at 1080p (4% apart), so the RTX 4080 Super is the quicker card at every resolution. Ray tracing widens the gap to 24% (75 vs 60 FPS at 1440p), as the RX 9070 XT's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 492 and 500 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K High the RX 9070 XT averages 74 FPS and the RTX 4080 Super 78, both playable without upscaling. Both carry 16 GB of VRAM, enough for 4K High. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need a Core i5-12400F or faster.

RX 9070 XT vs RTX 4080 Super: quick answers

Is the RX 9070 XT faster than the RTX 4080 Super?

No. The RTX 4080 Super is about 5% faster at 4K and 5% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 9070 XT gets about 60 FPS and the RTX 4080 Super 75 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 9070 XT and RTX 4080 Super get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 9070 XT averages about 169 FPS at 1080p, 122 at 1440p and 74 at 4K. The RTX 4080 Super averages 175, 128 and 78 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 9070 XT or RTX 4080 Super?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 9070 XT needs at least a Core i5-12400F and the RTX 4080 Super needs at least a Core i5-12400F. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 4080 Super and the RX 9070 XT.
