RTX 4060 Ti 8GB vs RTX 3070: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 4060 Ti 8GB and RTX 3070 are effectively tied: at 1440p High they average 59 and 59 FPS in AAA games with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and at 4K the gap is just 0%. At 1080p they're level too. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 32 and 30 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 294 and 294 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB manages 33 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 48. Both carry 8 GB of VRAM, which is already tight at 1440p High in new games. With frame rates this close, price and the features you value should decide, not benchmarks. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

RTX 4060 Ti 8GB vs RTX 3070: quick answers

Is the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB faster than the RTX 3070?

They're within 0% of each other at 4K and 0% at 1440p, so in practice they perform the same. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB gets about 32 FPS and the RTX 3070 30 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB and RTX 3070 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB averages about 92 FPS at 1080p, 59 at 1440p and 33 at 4K. The RTX 3070 averages 92, 59 and 33 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB or RTX 3070?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RTX 3070 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine.
