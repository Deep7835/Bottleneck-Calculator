Ryzen 5 9600X vs Core Ultra 5 245K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

You won't feel a difference between the Ryzen 5 9600X and Core Ultra 5 245K in most games. With an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, our model has them at 148 vs 144 FPS. It's a direct AMD vs Intel matchup between two 2024 chips. In esports games like CS2 it's 385 vs 374 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 81 vs 79 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 104 vs 101 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 101 FPS, effectively the same. The Ryzen 5 9600X sits on AM5 and the Core Ultra 5 245K on LGA1851, so switching means a new motherboard. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. The Core Ultra 5 245K has more threads (14 vs 12), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming.

Which should you buy?

For gaming this is a wash, so the decision comes down to price, platform and what else you do with the PC. If you stream or render too, the Core Ultra 5 245K's extra threads tip the balance.

Buy the Ryzen 5 9600X if…

  • you plan a faster graphics card: it keeps up with GPUs up to the RTX 4090 at 1440p.
  • you're building new: AM5 is the platform AMD's current Ryzen CPUs use.

Buy the Core Ultra 5 245K if…

  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 14 cores / 14 threads vs 6 / 12.
  • you pair it with a mid-range card: with an RTX 5070 at 1440p the two are level.

Ryzen 5 9600X vs Core Ultra 5 245K: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 5 9600X better than the Core Ultra 5 245K for gaming?

They're within 3% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 5 9600X and Core Ultra 5 245K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 5 9600X averages about 148 FPS at 1080p and 146 at 1440p in AAA games, and 385 FPS in esports titles. The Core Ultra 5 245K averages 144, 143 and 374 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 5 9600X or Core Ultra 5 245K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 5 9600X keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090 and the Core Ultra 5 245K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
