RTX 4090 vs RTX 3090 Ti: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 4090 sits in a higher performance class: 158 FPS at 1440p High against 101 for the RTX 3090 Ti with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and a 59% lead at 4K. At 1080p the lead shrinks to 34%, because even a 9800X3D can't feed the RTX 4090 fast enough there, so it earns its keep at 1440p and 4K. Ray tracing widens the gap to 67% (93 vs 56 FPS at 1440p), as the RTX 3090 Ti's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 516 and 445 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K High the RTX 4090 averages 97 FPS and the RTX 3090 Ti 61, both playable without upscaling. Both carry 24 GB of VRAM, enough for 4K High. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RTX 4090 needs a Core i5-14600K and the RTX 3090 Ti a Ryzen 5 5600.

RTX 4090 vs RTX 3090 Ti: quick answers

Is the RTX 4090 faster than the RTX 3090 Ti?

Yes. The RTX 4090 is about 59% faster at 4K and 56% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 4090 gets about 93 FPS and the RTX 3090 Ti 56 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 4090 and RTX 3090 Ti get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 4090 averages about 193 FPS at 1080p, 158 at 1440p and 97 at 4K. The RTX 3090 Ti averages 144, 101 and 61 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 4090 or RTX 3090 Ti?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 4090 needs at least a Core i5-14600K and the RTX 3090 Ti needs at least a Ryzen 5 5600. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 4090 and the RTX 3090 Ti.
