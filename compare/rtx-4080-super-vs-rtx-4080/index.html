RTX 4080 Super vs RTX 4080: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 4080 Super and RTX 4080 are effectively tied: at 1440p High they average 128 and 125 FPS in AAA games with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and at 4K the gap is just 3%. At 1080p they're 2% apart too. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 75 and 73 FPS respectively. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 500 and 496 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K High the RTX 4080 Super averages 78 FPS and the RTX 4080 76, both playable without upscaling. Both carry 16 GB of VRAM, enough for 4K High. With frame rates this close, price and the features you value should decide, not benchmarks. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need a Core i5-12400F or faster.

RTX 4080 Super vs RTX 4080: quick answers

Is the RTX 4080 Super faster than the RTX 4080?

They're within 3% of each other at 4K and 2% at 1440p, so in practice they perform the same. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 4080 Super gets about 75 FPS and the RTX 4080 73 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 4080 Super and RTX 4080 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 4080 Super averages about 175 FPS at 1080p, 128 at 1440p and 78 at 4K. The RTX 4080 averages 172, 125 and 76 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 4080 Super or RTX 4080?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 4080 Super needs at least a Core i5-12400F and the RTX 4080 needs at least a Core i5-12400F. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine.
