RTX 3080 vs RTX 3070: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 3080 sits in a higher performance class: 88 FPS at 1440p High against 59 for the RTX 3070 with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and a 54% lead at 4K. The order holds at 1080p (37% apart), so the RTX 3080 is the quicker card at every resolution. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 46 and 30 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 398 and 294 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 3070 manages 33 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 48. The RTX 3080 also has more memory (10 GB vs 8 GB), and 8 GB is already tight at 1440p High in new games. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

RTX 3080 vs RTX 3070: quick answers

Is the RTX 3080 faster than the RTX 3070?

Yes. The RTX 3080 is about 54% faster at 4K and 49% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 3080 gets about 46 FPS and the RTX 3070 30 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 3080 and RTX 3070 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 3080 averages about 126 FPS at 1080p, 88 at 1440p and 51 at 4K. The RTX 3070 averages 92, 59 and 33 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 3080 or RTX 3070?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 3080 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RTX 3070 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 3080 and the RTX 3070.
