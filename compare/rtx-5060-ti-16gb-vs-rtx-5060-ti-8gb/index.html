RTX 5060 Ti 16GB vs RTX 5060 Ti 8GB: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at 1440p High, the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB averages 77 FPS in AAA games to the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB's 70. That 10% lead grows to 20% at 4K. The order holds at 1080p (2% apart), so the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB is the quicker card at every resolution. Ray tracing widens the gap to 20% (45 vs 37 FPS at 1440p), as ray tracing needs extra VRAM. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 351 and 344 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB manages 39 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 56. The RTX 5060 Ti 16GB also has more memory (16 GB vs 8 GB), and 8 GB is already tight at 1440p High in new games. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

RTX 5060 Ti 16GB vs RTX 5060 Ti 8GB: quick answers

Is the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB faster than the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB?

Yes. The RTX 5060 Ti 16GB is about 20% faster at 4K and 10% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB gets about 45 FPS and the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB 37 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB and RTX 5060 Ti 8GB get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB averages about 110 FPS at 1080p, 77 at 1440p and 47 at 4K. The RTX 5060 Ti 8GB averages 108, 70 and 39 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB or RTX 5060 Ti 8GB?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB and the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB.
