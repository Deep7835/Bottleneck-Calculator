Core i5-14600K vs Core i5-13600K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

You won't feel a difference between the Core i5-14600K and Core i5-13600K in most games. With an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, our model has them at 150 vs 146 FPS. In esports games like CS2 it's 390 vs 380 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 82 vs 80 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 105 vs 102 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 101 FPS, effectively the same. Both use LGA1700, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. Both have 14 cores and 20 threads. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 4090 at 1440p.

Core i5-14600K vs Core i5-13600K: quick answers

Is the Core i5-14600K better than the Core i5-13600K for gaming?

They're within 3% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Core i5-14600K and Core i5-13600K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Core i5-14600K averages about 150 FPS at 1080p and 148 at 1440p in AAA games, and 390 FPS in esports titles. The Core i5-13600K averages 146, 145 and 380 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Core i5-14600K or Core i5-13600K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Core i5-14600K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090 and the Core i5-13600K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
