RX 9070 XT vs RX 7900 XTX: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RX 7900 XTX edges out the RX 9070 XT by 8% at 4K and 8% at 1440p, where it averages 131 FPS to 122 in AAA games at High settings with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D. Newer isn't faster here: the RX 7900 XTX came out 3 years before the RX 9070 XT and still leads in raster performance. The order holds at 1080p (5% apart), so the RX 7900 XTX is the quicker card at every resolution. Turn on ray tracing and the order flips: the RX 9070 XT leads 60 to 54 FPS at 1440p, because the RX 7900 XTX's RT hardware is less efficient. At 4K High the RX 9070 XT averages 74 FPS and the RX 7900 XTX 80, both playable without upscaling. The RX 7900 XTX also has more memory (24 GB vs 16 GB), though both have enough for 4K High today. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need a Core i5-12400F or faster.

Which should you buy?

Buy the RX 7900 XTX if you want the faster card: it's 8% ahead at 4K. The RX 9070 XT is the better buy only if it costs at least 8% less, the gap at 1440p, because then it delivers more frames per dollar. Ray tracing is the exception: there the RX 9070 XT leads by 13%. Already own the RX 9070 XT? The RX 7900 XTX is only 8% faster at 1440p and is older hardware, so switching is rarely worth it.

Buy the RX 9070 XT if…

  • you play with ray tracing: 60 vs 54 FPS at 1440p.
  • you want the newer card (2025) with the longer driver and feature support ahead of it.
  • it costs at least 8% less than the RX 7900 XTX, which makes it the better value per frame.

Buy the RX 7900 XTX if…

RX 9070 XT vs RX 7900 XTX: quick answers

Is the RX 9070 XT faster than the RX 7900 XTX?

No. The RX 7900 XTX is about 8% faster at 4K and 8% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 9070 XT gets about 60 FPS and the RX 7900 XTX 54 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 9070 XT and RX 7900 XTX get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 9070 XT averages about 169 FPS at 1080p, 122 at 1440p and 74 at 4K. The RX 7900 XTX averages 177, 131 and 80 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 9070 XT or RX 7900 XTX?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 9070 XT needs at least a Core i5-12400F and the RX 7900 XTX needs at least a Core i5-12400F. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RX 7900 XTX and the RX 9070 XT.
