RTX 5060 vs Arc B580: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 5060 and Arc B580 are effectively tied: at 1440p High they average 58 and 55 FPS in AAA games with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and at 4K the gap is just 3%. It's a cross-brand matchup: NVIDIA's 2025 card against Intel's 2024 card. At 1080p they're 15% apart too. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 31 and 29 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 287 and 250 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 5060 manages 32 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 46. The Arc B580 also has more memory (12 GB vs 8 GB), and 8 GB is already tight at 1440p High in new games. With frame rates this close, price and the features you value should decide, not benchmarks. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

Which should you buy?

On raster performance this is a coin toss, so buy whichever is cheaper where you shop. If you play with ray tracing, the RTX 5060 is the safer pick (8% faster with RT at 1440p). At the same price, take the Arc B580 for its extra VRAM.

Buy the RTX 5060 if…

  • you play with ray tracing: 31 vs 29 FPS at 1440p.
  • you want the newer card (2025) with the longer driver and feature support ahead of it.

Buy the Arc B580 if…

  • you play at 4K or use high-resolution texture packs: 12 GB of VRAM vs 8 GB.
  • it costs at least 6% less than the RTX 5060, which makes it the better value per frame.

RTX 5060 vs Arc B580: quick answers

Is the RTX 5060 faster than the Arc B580?

They're within 3% of each other at 4K and 6% at 1440p, so in practice they perform the same. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 5060 gets about 31 FPS and the Arc B580 29 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 5060 and Arc B580 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 5060 averages about 90 FPS at 1080p, 58 at 1440p and 32 at 4K. The Arc B580 averages 78, 55 and 33 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 5060 or Arc B580?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 5060 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the Arc B580 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine.
