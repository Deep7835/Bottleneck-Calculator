RTX 4070 vs RTX 3080: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 4070 edges out the RTX 3080 by 9% at 4K and 4% at 1440p, where it averages 84 FPS to 80 in AAA games at High settings with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D. For a card 3 years newer, that is a modest generational step. The order holds at 1080p (4% apart), so the RTX 4070 is the quicker card at every resolution. Ray tracing widens the gap to 15% (48 vs 42 FPS at 1440p), as the RTX 3080's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 379 and 365 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 3080 manages 46 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 66. The RTX 4070 also has more memory (12 GB vs 10 GB), and 10 GB is short of the ~11 GB that 4K High needs. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

Which should you buy?

Buy the RTX 4070 if you want the faster card: it's 9% ahead at 4K. The RTX 3080 is the better buy only if it costs at least 4% less, the gap at 1440p, because then it delivers more frames per dollar. Already own the RTX 3080? Going to the RTX 4070 gains 4% at 1440p, which is too small to justify replacing it.

Buy the RTX 4070 if…

  • you want the most FPS: it's 9% faster at 4K and 4% at 1440p.
  • you play with ray tracing: 48 vs 42 FPS at 1440p.
  • you play at 4K or use high-resolution texture packs: 12 GB of VRAM vs 10 GB.
  • you want the newer card (2023) with the longer driver and feature support ahead of it.

Buy the RTX 3080 if…

  • it costs at least 4% less than the RTX 4070, which makes it the better value per frame.
  • you play at 1080p, where the gap is only 4% and upscaling hides it further.

RTX 4070 vs RTX 3080: quick answers

Is the RTX 4070 faster than the RTX 3080?

Yes. The RTX 4070 is about 9% faster at 4K and 4% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 4070 gets about 48 FPS and the RTX 3080 42 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 4070 and RTX 3080 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 4070 averages about 119 FPS at 1080p, 84 at 1440p and 51 at 4K. The RTX 3080 averages 115, 80 and 46 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 4070 or RTX 3080?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 4070 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RTX 3080 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 4070 and the RTX 3080.
