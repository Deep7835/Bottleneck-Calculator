RTX 3060 12GB vs GTX 1080 Ti: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The GTX 1080 Ti edges out the RTX 3060 12GB by 7% at 4K and 7% at 1440p, where it averages 48 FPS to 45 in AAA games at High settings with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D. Newer isn't faster here: the GTX 1080 Ti came out 4 years before the RTX 3060 12GB and still leads in raster performance. The order holds at 1080p (7% apart), so the GTX 1080 Ti is the quicker card at every resolution. The GTX 1080 Ti has no ray tracing hardware, so with RT on at 1440p it drops to 6 FPS against 25 for the RTX 3060 12GB. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 206 and 221 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor. The RTX 3060 12GB also has more memory (12 GB vs 11 GB), though both have enough for 4K High today. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

Which should you buy?

Buy the GTX 1080 Ti if you want the faster card: it's 7% ahead at 4K. The RTX 3060 12GB is the better buy only if it costs at least 7% less, the gap at 1440p, because then it delivers more frames per dollar. Ray tracing is the exception: there the RTX 3060 12GB leads by 303%. Already own the RTX 3060 12GB? The GTX 1080 Ti is only 7% faster at 1440p and is older hardware, so switching is rarely worth it.

Buy the RTX 3060 12GB if…

  • you play with ray tracing: 25 vs 6 FPS at 1440p.
  • you play at 4K or use high-resolution texture packs: 12 GB of VRAM vs 11 GB.
  • you want the newer card (2021) with the longer driver and feature support ahead of it.
  • it costs at least 7% less than the GTX 1080 Ti, which makes it the better value per frame.

Buy the GTX 1080 Ti if…

  • you want the most FPS: it's 7% faster at 4K and 7% at 1440p.
  • it's the cheaper of the two where you shop: performance is close enough that price decides.

RTX 3060 12GB vs GTX 1080 Ti: quick answers

Is the RTX 3060 12GB faster than the GTX 1080 Ti?

No. The GTX 1080 Ti is about 7% faster at 4K and 7% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 3060 12GB gets about 25 FPS and the GTX 1080 Ti 6 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 3060 12GB and GTX 1080 Ti get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 3060 12GB averages about 64 FPS at 1080p, 45 at 1440p and 27 at 4K. The GTX 1080 Ti averages 69, 48 and 29 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 3060 12GB or GTX 1080 Ti?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 3060 12GB needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the GTX 1080 Ti needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the GTX 1080 Ti and the RTX 3060 12GB.
