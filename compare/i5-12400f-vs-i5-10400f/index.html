Core i5-12400F vs Core i5-10400F: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The Core i5-12400F is in a different league: 36% faster than the Core i5-10400F at 1080p with an RTX 5090, or 120 vs 88 FPS in AAA games at High settings. The gap holds at 1440p (36%) because even there the CPU, not the RTX 5090, sets the frame rate. In esports games like CS2 it's 312 vs 229 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 66 vs 48 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 84 vs 62 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 98 and 85 FPS, 16% apart. The Core i5-12400F sits on LGA1700 and the Core i5-10400F on LGA1200, so switching means a new motherboard. Both have 6 cores and 12 threads. Under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the Core i5-12400F can drive up to the RX 7900 XTX and the Core i5-10400F up to the RTX 4070 Super.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Core i5-12400F if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 36% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card. The Core i5-10400F makes sense if it costs at least 36% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Core i5-10400F? Going to the Core i5-12400F gains 36% at 1080p, which is a worthwhile upgrade. It also needs a new LGA1700 motherboard.

Buy the Core i5-12400F if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 36% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 84 vs 62 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 66 vs 48 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you plan a faster graphics card: it keeps up with GPUs up to the RX 7900 XTX at 1440p.
  • you already own an LGA1700 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.

Buy the Core i5-10400F if…

  • it costs at least 36% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.
  • it's the cheaper of the two where you shop: gaming performance is close enough that price decides.

Core i5-12400F vs Core i5-10400F: quick answers

Is the Core i5-12400F better than the Core i5-10400F for gaming?

Yes. The Core i5-12400F is about 36% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 36% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Core i5-12400F and Core i5-10400F get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Core i5-12400F averages about 120 FPS at 1080p and 120 at 1440p in AAA games, and 312 FPS in esports titles. The Core i5-10400F averages 88, 88 and 229 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Core i5-12400F or Core i5-10400F?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Core i5-12400F keeps up with cards up to the RX 7900 XTX and the Core i5-10400F keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4070 Super. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
