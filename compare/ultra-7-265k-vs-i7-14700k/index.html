Core Ultra 7 265K vs Core i7-14700K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

With an RTX 5090 taking the graphics card out of the equation, the Core i7-14700K averages 158 FPS at 1080p High to the Core Ultra 7 265K's 150, 5% more. Newer isn't faster here: the Core i7-14700K is 1 year older and still wins in games. The gap holds at 1440p (5%) because even there the CPU, not the RTX 5090, sets the frame rate. In esports games like CS2 it's 390 vs 411 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 82 vs 87 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 105 vs 110 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 101 FPS, effectively the same. The Core Ultra 7 265K sits on LGA1851 and the Core i7-14700K on LGA1700, so switching means a new motherboard. The Core i7-14700K has more threads (28 vs 20), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 4090 at 1440p.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Core i7-14700K if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 5% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card. The Core Ultra 7 265K makes sense if it costs at least 5% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Core Ultra 7 265K? The Core i7-14700K is only 5% faster at 1080p and is older hardware, so switching is rarely worth it.

Buy the Core Ultra 7 265K if…

  • it costs at least 5% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.
  • you pair it with a mid-range card: with an RTX 5070 at 1440p the two are level.

Buy the Core i7-14700K if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 5% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 110 vs 105 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 87 vs 82 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 20 cores / 28 threads vs 20 / 20.
  • you already own an LGA1700 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.

Core Ultra 7 265K vs Core i7-14700K: quick answers

Is the Core Ultra 7 265K better than the Core i7-14700K for gaming?

No. The Core i7-14700K is about 5% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 5% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Core Ultra 7 265K and Core i7-14700K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Core Ultra 7 265K averages about 150 FPS at 1080p and 148 at 1440p in AAA games, and 390 FPS in esports titles. The Core i7-14700K averages 158, 155 and 411 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Core Ultra 7 265K or Core i7-14700K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Core Ultra 7 265K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090 and the Core i7-14700K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
