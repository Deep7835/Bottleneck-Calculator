RX 7700 XT vs RTX 4060 Ti 8GB: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RX 7700 XT sits in a higher performance class: 76 FPS at 1440p High against 59 for the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and a 38% lead at 4K. Both launched in 2023, so this is a straight AMD vs NVIDIA fight for the same buyers. The order holds at 1080p (17% apart), so the RX 7700 XT is the quicker card at every resolution. Turn on ray tracing and the order flips: the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB leads 32 to 31 FPS at 1440p, because the RX 7700 XT's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 344 and 294 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor. The RX 7700 XT also has more memory (12 GB vs 8 GB), and 8 GB is already tight at 1440p High in new games. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need only a Ryzen 5 3600-class CPU.

RX 7700 XT vs RTX 4060 Ti 8GB: quick answers

Is the RX 7700 XT faster than the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB?

Yes. The RX 7700 XT is about 38% faster at 4K and 27% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 7700 XT gets about 31 FPS and the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB 32 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 7700 XT and RTX 4060 Ti 8GB get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 7700 XT averages about 108 FPS at 1080p, 76 at 1440p and 46 at 4K. The RTX 4060 Ti 8GB averages 92, 59 and 33 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 7700 XT or RTX 4060 Ti 8GB?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 7700 XT needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster and the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RX 7700 XT and the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB.
