RTX 5070 vs RTX 4070: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at 1440p High, the RTX 5070 averages 101 FPS in AAA games to the RTX 4070's 84. That 21% lead is about the same at 4K. For a card 2 years newer, that is a solid generational step. The order holds at 1080p (20% apart), so the RTX 5070 is the quicker card at every resolution. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 59 and 48 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 445 and 379 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 4070 manages 51 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 71. Both carry 12 GB of VRAM, enough for 4K High. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RTX 5070 needs a Ryzen 5 5600 and the RTX 4070 a Ryzen 5 3600-class chip.

RTX 5070 vs RTX 4070: quick answers

Is the RTX 5070 faster than the RTX 4070?

Yes. The RTX 5070 is about 21% faster at 4K and 21% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 5070 gets about 59 FPS and the RTX 4070 48 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 5070 and RTX 4070 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 5070 averages about 144 FPS at 1080p, 101 at 1440p and 61 at 4K. The RTX 4070 averages 119, 84 and 51 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 5070 or RTX 4070?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 5070 needs at least a Ryzen 5 5600 and the RTX 4070 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 5070 and the RTX 4070.
