RTX 5070 Ti vs RTX 3080: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 5070 Ti sits in a higher performance class: 123 FPS at 1440p High against 88 for the RTX 3080 with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and a 47% lead at 4K. For a card 5 years newer, that is a big generational step. The order holds at 1080p (35% apart), so the RTX 5070 Ti is the quicker card at every resolution. Ray tracing widens the gap to 55% (72 vs 46 FPS at 1440p), as the RTX 3080's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 494 and 398 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 3080 manages 51 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 73. The RTX 5070 Ti also has more memory (16 GB vs 10 GB), and 10 GB is short of the ~11 GB that 4K High needs. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RTX 5070 Ti needs a Core i5-12400F and the RTX 3080 a Ryzen 5 3600-class chip.

RTX 5070 Ti vs RTX 3080: quick answers

Is the RTX 5070 Ti faster than the RTX 3080?

Yes. The RTX 5070 Ti is about 47% faster at 4K and 40% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 5070 Ti gets about 72 FPS and the RTX 3080 46 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 5070 Ti and RTX 3080 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 5070 Ti averages about 170 FPS at 1080p, 123 at 1440p and 75 at 4K. The RTX 3080 averages 126, 88 and 51 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 5070 Ti or RTX 3080?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 5070 Ti needs at least a Core i5-12400F and the RTX 3080 needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 5070 Ti and the RTX 3080.
