RX 9070 XT vs RX 7900 XT: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RX 9070 XT edges out the RX 7900 XT by 9% at 4K and 8% at 1440p, where it averages 122 FPS to 112 in AAA games at High settings with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D. For a card 3 years newer, that is a modest generational step. The order holds at 1080p (7% apart), so the RX 9070 XT is the quicker card at every resolution. Ray tracing widens the gap to 32% (60 vs 46 FPS at 1440p), as the RX 7900 XT's RT hardware is less efficient. At 4K High the RX 9070 XT averages 74 FPS and the RX 7900 XT 68, both playable without upscaling. The RX 7900 XT also has more memory (20 GB vs 16 GB), though both have enough for 4K High today. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RX 9070 XT needs a Core i5-12400F and the RX 7900 XT a Ryzen 5 5600.

Buy the RX 9070 XT if you want the faster card: it's 9% ahead at 4K. RX 9070 XT vs RX 7900 XT: quick answers

Is the RX 9070 XT faster than the RX 7900 XT?

Yes. The RX 9070 XT is about 9% faster at 4K and 8% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 9070 XT gets about 60 FPS and the RX 7900 XT 46 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 9070 XT and RX 7900 XT get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 9070 XT averages about 169 FPS at 1080p, 122 at 1440p and 74 at 4K. The RX 7900 XT averages 158, 112 and 68 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 9070 XT or RX 7900 XT?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 9070 XT needs at least a Core i5-12400F and the RX 7900 XT needs at least a Ryzen 5 5600. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RX 9070 XT and the RX 7900 XT.
