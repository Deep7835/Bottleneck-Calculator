Ryzen 5 5600 vs Ryzen 5 5600X: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The Ryzen 5 5600 and Ryzen 5 5600X are neck and neck in games: paired with an RTX 5090 at 1080p High they average 106 and 108 FPS in AAA titles, a 2% difference. In esports games like CS2 it's 276 vs 281 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 58 vs 59 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 74 vs 76 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 95 and 95 FPS, effectively the same. Both use AM4, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap after a BIOS update. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. Both have 6 cores and 12 threads. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 4070 Ti Super at 1440p.

Ryzen 5 5600 vs Ryzen 5 5600X: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 5 5600 better than the Ryzen 5 5600X for gaming?

They're within 2% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 5 5600 and Ryzen 5 5600X get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 5 5600 averages about 106 FPS at 1080p and 106 at 1440p in AAA games, and 276 FPS in esports titles. The Ryzen 5 5600X averages 108, 108 and 281 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 5 5600 or Ryzen 5 5600X?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 5 5600 keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4070 Ti Super and the Ryzen 5 5600X keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4070 Ti Super. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
