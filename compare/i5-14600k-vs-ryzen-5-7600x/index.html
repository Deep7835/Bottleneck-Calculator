Core i5-14600K vs Ryzen 5 7600X: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

With an RTX 5090 taking the graphics card out of the equation, the Core i5-14600K averages 150 FPS at 1080p High to the Ryzen 5 7600X's 140, 7% more. It's a direct AMD vs Intel matchup. The gap holds at 1440p (7%) because even there the CPU, not the RTX 5090, sets the frame rate. In esports games like CS2 it's 390 vs 364 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 82 vs 77 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 105 vs 98 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 100 FPS, effectively the same. The Core i5-14600K sits on LGA1700 and the Ryzen 5 7600X on AM5, so switching means a new motherboard. The Core i5-14600K has more threads (20 vs 12), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming. Under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the Core i5-14600K can drive up to the RTX 4090 and the Ryzen 5 7600X up to the RTX 5080.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Core i5-14600K if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 7% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card. The Ryzen 5 7600X makes sense if it costs at least 7% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Ryzen 5 7600X? Going to the Core i5-14600K gains 7% at 1080p, which is too small to justify replacing it. It also needs a new LGA1700 motherboard.

Buy the Core i5-14600K if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 7% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 105 vs 98 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 82 vs 77 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 14 cores / 20 threads vs 6 / 12.
  • you plan a faster graphics card: it keeps up with GPUs up to the RTX 4090 at 1440p.
  • you already own an LGA1700 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.

Buy the Ryzen 5 7600X if…

  • you're building new: AM5 is the platform AMD's current Ryzen CPUs use.
  • it costs at least 7% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.

Core i5-14600K vs Ryzen 5 7600X: quick answers

Is the Core i5-14600K better than the Ryzen 5 7600X for gaming?

Yes. The Core i5-14600K is about 7% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 7% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Core i5-14600K and Ryzen 5 7600X get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Core i5-14600K averages about 150 FPS at 1080p and 148 at 1440p in AAA games, and 390 FPS in esports titles. The Ryzen 5 7600X averages 140, 139 and 364 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Core i5-14600K or Ryzen 5 7600X?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Core i5-14600K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090 and the Ryzen 5 7600X keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
