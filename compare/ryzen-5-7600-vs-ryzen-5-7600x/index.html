Ryzen 5 7600 vs Ryzen 5 7600X: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The Ryzen 5 7600 and Ryzen 5 7600X are neck and neck in games: paired with an RTX 5090 at 1080p High they average 136 and 140 FPS in AAA titles, a 3% difference. In esports games like CS2 it's 354 vs 364 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 75 vs 77 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 95 vs 98 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 100 and 100 FPS, effectively the same. Both use AM5, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap after a BIOS update. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. Both have 6 cores and 12 threads. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 5080 at 1440p.

Ryzen 5 7600 vs Ryzen 5 7600X: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 5 7600 better than the Ryzen 5 7600X for gaming?

They're within 3% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 5 7600 and Ryzen 5 7600X get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 5 7600 averages about 136 FPS at 1080p and 135 at 1440p in AAA games, and 354 FPS in esports titles. The Ryzen 5 7600X averages 140, 139 and 364 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 5 7600 or Ryzen 5 7600X?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 5 7600 keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080 and the Ryzen 5 7600X keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
