Ryzen 7 5800X3D vs Core i9-12900K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

You won't feel a difference between the Ryzen 7 5800X3D and Core i9-12900K in most games. With an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, our model has them at 140 vs 138 FPS. It's a direct AMD vs Intel matchup. In esports games like CS2 it's 364 vs 359 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 77 vs 76 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 104 vs 97 FPS, helped by the Ryzen 7 5800X3D's large cache. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 100 and 100 FPS, effectively the same. The Ryzen 7 5800X3D sits on AM4 and the Core i9-12900K on LGA1700, so switching means a new motherboard. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. The Core i9-12900K has more threads (24 vs 16), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 5080 at 1440p.

Which should you buy?

For gaming this is a wash, so the decision comes down to price, platform and what else you do with the PC. If you stream or render too, the Core i9-12900K's extra threads tip the balance.

Buy the Ryzen 7 5800X3D if…

  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 104 vs 97 FPS at 1080p.
  • you already own an AM4 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.

Buy the Core i9-12900K if…

  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 16 cores / 24 threads vs 8 / 16.
  • you already own an LGA1700 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.

Ryzen 7 5800X3D vs Core i9-12900K: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 7 5800X3D better than the Core i9-12900K for gaming?

They're within 1% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 7 5800X3D and Core i9-12900K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 7 5800X3D averages about 140 FPS at 1080p and 139 at 1440p in AAA games, and 364 FPS in esports titles. The Core i9-12900K averages 138, 137 and 359 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 7 5800X3D or Core i9-12900K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 7 5800X3D keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080 and the Core i9-12900K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
