RX 9070 XT vs RX 9070: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at 1440p High, the RX 9070 XT averages 122 FPS in AAA games to the RX 9070's 106. That 15% lead is about the same at 4K. The order holds at 1080p (12% apart), so the RX 9070 XT is the quicker card at every resolution. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 60 and 52 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 492 and 459 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K High the RX 9070 XT averages 74 FPS and the RX 9070 64, both playable without upscaling. Both carry 16 GB of VRAM, enough for 4K High. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RX 9070 XT needs a Core i5-12400F and the RX 9070 a Ryzen 5 5600.

RX 9070 XT vs RX 9070: quick answers

Is the RX 9070 XT faster than the RX 9070?

Yes. The RX 9070 XT is about 15% faster at 4K and 15% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 9070 XT gets about 60 FPS and the RX 9070 52 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 9070 XT and RX 9070 get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 9070 XT averages about 169 FPS at 1080p, 122 at 1440p and 74 at 4K. The RX 9070 averages 150, 106 and 64 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 9070 XT or RX 9070?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 9070 XT needs at least a Core i5-12400F and the RX 9070 needs at least a Ryzen 5 5600. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RX 9070 XT and the RX 9070.
