RX 7900 XTX vs RX 7900 XT: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RX 7900 XTX is clearly the faster card: 17% ahead of the RX 7900 XT at 4K and 17% at 1440p, where it averages 131 FPS to 112 in AAA games at High settings with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D. The order holds at 1080p (13% apart), so the RX 7900 XTX is the quicker card at every resolution. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 54 and 46 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 503 and 475 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K High the RX 7900 XTX averages 80 FPS and the RX 7900 XT 68, both playable without upscaling. The RX 7900 XTX also has more memory (24 GB vs 20 GB), though both have enough for 4K High today. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RX 7900 XTX needs a Core i5-12400F and the RX 7900 XT a Ryzen 5 5600.

RX 7900 XTX vs RX 7900 XT: quick answers

Is the RX 7900 XTX faster than the RX 7900 XT?

Yes. The RX 7900 XTX is about 17% faster at 4K and 17% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RX 7900 XTX gets about 54 FPS and the RX 7900 XT 46 FPS.

How many FPS do the RX 7900 XTX and RX 7900 XT get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RX 7900 XTX averages about 177 FPS at 1080p, 131 at 1440p and 80 at 4K. The RX 7900 XT averages 158, 112 and 68 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RX 7900 XTX or RX 7900 XT?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RX 7900 XTX needs at least a Core i5-12400F and the RX 7900 XT needs at least a Ryzen 5 5600. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RX 7900 XTX and the RX 7900 XT.
