Ryzen 7 9800X3D vs Core i9-14900K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

Paired with an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, the Ryzen 7 9800X3D pushes 199 FPS in AAA games against 160 for the Core i9-14900K, a clear 24% advantage. The Ryzen 7 9800X3D's 3D V-Cache is the reason: the extra L3 cache keeps more game data close to the cores. At 1440p the gap narrows to 17% as the RTX 5090 starts to set the pace; with a mid-range card it would shrink further. In esports games like CS2 it's 519 vs 416 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 110 vs 88 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 147 vs 112 FPS, helped by the Ryzen 7 9800X3D's large cache. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 101 FPS, effectively the same. The Ryzen 7 9800X3D sits on AM5 and the Core i9-14900K on LGA1700, so switching means a new motherboard. The Core i9-14900K has more threads (32 vs 16), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Ryzen 7 9800X3D if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 24% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card (17% at 1440p). The Core i9-14900K makes sense if it costs at least 17% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Core i9-14900K? Going to the Ryzen 7 9800X3D gains 24% at 1080p, which is a modest upgrade, worth it mainly if you can sell the Core i9-14900K. It also needs a new AM5 motherboard.

Buy the Ryzen 7 9800X3D if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 24% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 147 vs 112 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 110 vs 88 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you plan a faster graphics card: it keeps up with GPUs up to the RTX 5090 at 1440p.
  • you're building new: AM5 is the platform AMD's current Ryzen CPUs use.

Buy the Core i9-14900K if…

  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 24 cores / 32 threads vs 8 / 16.
  • you already own an LGA1700 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.
  • it costs at least 24% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.

Ryzen 7 9800X3D vs Core i9-14900K: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 7 9800X3D better than the Core i9-14900K for gaming?

Yes. The Ryzen 7 9800X3D is about 24% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 17% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 7 9800X3D and Core i9-14900K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 7 9800X3D averages about 199 FPS at 1080p and 183 at 1440p in AAA games, and 519 FPS in esports titles. The Core i9-14900K averages 160, 157 and 416 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 7 9800X3D or Core i9-14900K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 7 9800X3D keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5090 and the Core i9-14900K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
