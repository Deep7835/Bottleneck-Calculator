Core i5-13600K vs Core i5-12600K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

Paired with an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, the Core i5-13600K pushes 146 FPS in AAA games against 128 for the Core i5-12600K, a clear 14% advantage. The gap holds at 1440p (13%) because even there the CPU, not the RTX 5090, sets the frame rate. In esports games like CS2 it's 380 vs 333 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 80 vs 70 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 102 vs 90 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 99 FPS, effectively the same. Both use LGA1700, so moving from one to the other is a drop-in swap. The Core i5-13600K has more threads (20 vs 16), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming. Under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the Core i5-13600K can drive up to the RTX 4090 and the Core i5-12600K up to the RTX 5080.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Core i5-13600K if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 14% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card. The Core i5-12600K makes sense if it costs at least 13% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Core i5-12600K? Going to the Core i5-13600K gains 14% at 1080p, which is a modest upgrade, worth it mainly if you can sell the Core i5-12600K. It drops into the same motherboard.

Buy the Core i5-13600K if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 14% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 102 vs 90 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 80 vs 70 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 14 cores / 20 threads vs 10 / 16.
  • you plan a faster graphics card: it keeps up with GPUs up to the RTX 4090 at 1440p.

Buy the Core i5-12600K if…

  • it costs at least 14% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.
  • you pair it with a mid-range card: with an RTX 5070 at 1440p the two are level.

Core i5-13600K vs Core i5-12600K: quick answers

Is the Core i5-13600K better than the Core i5-12600K for gaming?

Yes. The Core i5-13600K is about 14% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 13% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Core i5-13600K and Core i5-12600K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Core i5-13600K averages about 146 FPS at 1080p and 145 at 1440p in AAA games, and 380 FPS in esports titles. The Core i5-12600K averages 128, 128 and 333 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Core i5-13600K or Core i5-12600K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Core i5-13600K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090 and the Core i5-12600K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 5080. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
