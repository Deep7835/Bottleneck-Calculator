RTX 5070 vs RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

This is not a close contest. The RTX 5070 is 31% faster than the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB at 4K and 31% faster at 1440p: 101 vs 77 FPS in AAA games at High settings with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D. The order holds at 1080p (30% apart), so the RTX 5070 is the quicker card at every resolution. With ray tracing on at 1440p they manage 59 and 45 FPS respectively, so plan on DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 445 and 351 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB manages 47 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 65. The RTX 5060 Ti 16GB also has more memory (16 GB vs 12 GB), though both have enough for 4K High today. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, the RTX 5070 needs a Ryzen 5 5600 and the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB a Ryzen 5 3600-class chip.

RTX 5070 vs RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: quick answers

Is the RTX 5070 faster than the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB?

Yes. The RTX 5070 is about 31% faster at 4K and 31% faster at 1440p in AAA games at High settings. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 5070 gets about 59 FPS and the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB 45 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 5070 and RTX 5060 Ti 16GB get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 5070 averages about 144 FPS at 1080p, 101 at 1440p and 61 at 4K. The RTX 5060 Ti 16GB averages 110, 77 and 47 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 5070 or RTX 5060 Ti 16GB?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 5070 needs at least a Ryzen 5 5600 and the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB needs a Ryzen 5 3600 or faster. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine. A slower CPU narrows the gap between the RTX 5070 and the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB.
