Core Ultra 9 285K vs Core i9-14900K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

With an RTX 5090 taking the graphics card out of the equation, the Core i9-14900K averages 170 FPS at 1080p High to the Core Ultra 9 285K's 164, 4% more. Newer isn't faster here: the Core i9-14900K is 1 year older and still wins in games. The gap holds at 1440p (3%) because even there the CPU, not the RTX 5090, sets the frame rate. In esports games like CS2 it's 426 vs 442 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 90 vs 93 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 115 vs 119 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 101 FPS, effectively the same. The Core Ultra 9 285K sits on LGA1851 and the Core i9-14900K on LGA1700, so switching means a new motherboard. The Core i9-14900K has more threads (32 vs 24), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 4090 at 1440p.

Which should you buy?

Buy the Core i9-14900K if you want the faster gaming CPU: it's 4% ahead at 1080p with a high-end card. The Core Ultra 9 285K makes sense if it costs at least 3% less, or if you pair it with a mid-range GPU that hides the difference. Already own the Core Ultra 9 285K? The Core i9-14900K is only 4% faster at 1080p and is older hardware, so switching is rarely worth it.

Buy the Core Ultra 9 285K if…

  • it costs at least 4% less, or you game at 1440p and above where the gap shrinks.
  • you pair it with a mid-range card: with an RTX 5070 at 1440p the two are level.

Buy the Core i9-14900K if…

  • you play at high refresh rates: 4% more FPS at 1080p with a fast GPU.
  • you care about smoothness: 1% lows of 119 vs 115 FPS at 1080p.
  • you play strategy, sims or MMOs: 93 vs 90 FPS in CPU-heavy games.
  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 24 cores / 32 threads vs 24 / 24.
  • you already own an LGA1700 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.

Core Ultra 9 285K vs Core i9-14900K: quick answers

Is the Core Ultra 9 285K better than the Core i9-14900K for gaming?

No. The Core i9-14900K is about 4% faster at 1080p with an RTX 5090 and 3% faster at 1440p. The gap is smaller with a mid-range graphics card, which becomes the limit first.

How many FPS do the Core Ultra 9 285K and Core i9-14900K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Core Ultra 9 285K averages about 164 FPS at 1080p and 161 at 1440p in AAA games, and 426 FPS in esports titles. The Core i9-14900K averages 170, 167 and 442 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Core Ultra 9 285K or Core i9-14900K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Core Ultra 9 285K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090 and the Core i9-14900K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
