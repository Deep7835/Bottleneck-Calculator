Ryzen 7 9700X vs Core i7-14700K: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

You won't feel a difference between the Ryzen 7 9700X and Core i7-14700K in most games. With an RTX 5090 at 1080p High, our model has them at 154 vs 158 FPS. It's a direct AMD vs Intel matchup. In esports games like CS2 it's 400 vs 411 FPS, and in CPU-heavy titles such as Microsoft Flight Simulator 85 vs 87 FPS at 1440p. 1% lows, which decide how smooth a game feels, come in at 108 vs 110 FPS. With a more typical RTX 5070 at 1440p they land at 101 and 101 FPS, effectively the same. The Ryzen 7 9700X sits on AM5 and the Core i7-14700K on LGA1700, so switching means a new motherboard. With gaming performance this close, the price of the chip, the motherboard and the cooler matters more than the benchmark. The Core i7-14700K has more threads (28 vs 16), which helps streaming and productivity more than gaming. Both keep up with GPUs up to the RTX 4090 at 1440p.

Which should you buy?

For gaming this is a wash, so the decision comes down to price, platform and what else you do with the PC. If you stream or render too, the Core i7-14700K's extra threads tip the balance.

Buy the Ryzen 7 9700X if…

  • you're building new: AM5 is the platform AMD's current Ryzen CPUs use.
  • you pair it with a mid-range card: with an RTX 5070 at 1440p the two are level.

Buy the Core i7-14700K if…

  • you also stream, edit video or compile code: 20 cores / 28 threads vs 8 / 16.
  • you already own an LGA1700 motherboard: it's a drop-in upgrade with no new board or RAM.

Ryzen 7 9700X vs Core i7-14700K: quick answers

Is the Ryzen 7 9700X better than the Core i7-14700K for gaming?

They're within 3% of each other at 1080p with an RTX 5090, so for gaming they're effectively equal. Choose on price, platform and core count instead.

How many FPS do the Ryzen 7 9700X and Core i7-14700K get?

Paired with an RTX 5090 at High settings, the Ryzen 7 9700X averages about 154 FPS at 1080p and 152 at 1440p in AAA games, and 400 FPS in esports titles. The Core i7-14700K averages 158, 155 and 411 FPS.

Which graphics card should I pair with the Ryzen 7 9700X or Core i7-14700K?

For a CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the Ryzen 7 9700X keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090 and the Core i7-14700K keeps up with cards up to the RTX 4090. Any slower card is a safe match. At 4K both CPUs can drive faster GPUs, and at 1080p they need slower ones.
