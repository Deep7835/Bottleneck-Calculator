RTX 4070 Super vs RX 7900 GRE: Gaming Performance & Bottleneck (2026)

The RTX 4070 Super and RX 7900 GRE are effectively tied: at 1440p High they average 96 and 96 FPS in AAA games with a Ryzen 7 9800X3D, and at 4K the gap is just 0%. It's a cross-brand matchup: NVIDIA's 2024 card against AMD's 2023 card. At 1080p they're level too. Ray tracing widens the gap to 43% (56 vs 39 FPS at 1440p), as the RX 7900 GRE's RT hardware is less efficient. In esports games like CS2 or Valorant at 1080p they reach 429 and 429 FPS, so either can feed a 360 Hz monitor. At 4K the RTX 4070 Super manages 58 FPS native, and DLSS Quality lifts that to about 82. The RX 7900 GRE also has more memory (16 GB vs 12 GB), though both have enough for 4K High today. With frame rates this close, price and the features you value should decide, not benchmarks. To stay under a 10% CPU bottleneck at 1440p, both need a Core i5-10400F or faster.

Which should you buy?

On raster performance this is a coin toss, so buy whichever is cheaper where you shop. If you play with ray tracing, the RTX 4070 Super is the safer pick (43% faster with RT at 1440p). At the same price, take the RX 7900 GRE for its extra VRAM.

Buy the RTX 4070 Super if…

  • you play with ray tracing: 56 vs 39 FPS at 1440p.
  • you want the newer card (2024) with the longer driver and feature support ahead of it.

Buy the RX 7900 GRE if…

  • you play at 4K or use high-resolution texture packs: 16 GB of VRAM vs 12 GB.
  • it's the cheaper of the two where you shop: performance is close enough that price decides.

RTX 4070 Super vs RX 7900 GRE: quick answers

Is the RTX 4070 Super faster than the RX 7900 GRE?

They're within 0% of each other at 4K and 0% at 1440p, so in practice they perform the same. With ray tracing on at 1440p the RTX 4070 Super gets about 56 FPS and the RX 7900 GRE 39 FPS.

How many FPS do the RTX 4070 Super and RX 7900 GRE get?

With a Ryzen 7 9800X3D at High settings in AAA games, the RTX 4070 Super averages about 137 FPS at 1080p, 96 at 1440p and 58 at 4K. The RX 7900 GRE averages 137, 96 and 58 FPS. DLSS, FSR or XeSS upscaling adds roughly 40% where the graphics card is the limit.

What CPU do I need for the RTX 4070 Super or RX 7900 GRE?

To keep the CPU bottleneck under 10% at 1440p, the RTX 4070 Super needs at least a Core i5-10400F and the RX 7900 GRE needs at least a Core i5-10400F. At 1080p you need a faster CPU for the same result, and at 4K a slower one is fine.
