What to include

  • Wrong or surprising result: the CPU, GPU and settings you used (the Copy link button in the calculator captures all of them), and a link to the benchmark or your own numbers.
  • Missing hardware: the exact model name. We add parts once independent reviews are available.
  • Anything else: questions about the method, the data, or this site.

Before you write, the methodology and FAQ on the homepage answer most questions about how results are calculated. Our editorial policy explains how corrections are handled.
