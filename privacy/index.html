Privacy policy

Short version: the calculator runs entirely in your browser. We don't have accounts, we don't set cookies, and we don't store anything on your device.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What the calculator does with your choices

The CPU, graphics card and settings you pick are processed by JavaScript in your browser. They are never sent to us, because the site has no server-side code or database.

Your current selection is kept in the page address (for example ?cpu=ryzen-5-7600&gpu=rtx-5070) so you can bookmark or share a result. That link only contains hardware names and settings. When you press Copy link, the address is copied to your clipboard and goes nowhere else.

Cookies and local storage

This site doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't use localStorage, sessionStorage or any other browser storage. Nothing is remembered between visits.

Fonts

The site's typeface, Geist, is served from our own domain together with the rest of the site. No font requests go to Google or any other third party, so loading a page doesn't share your IP address with a font provider.

Hosting and server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider automatically processes standard request data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit, so it can serve pages and protect against abuse. We don't combine this data with anything else or use it to identify visitors.

Analytics and advertising

We don't currently use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

If we add them in future (for example a privacy-friendly analytics tool, Google Analytics or Google AdSense), we'll update this page before they go live. It will name each provider, explain what it collects and whether it uses cookies, and link to its own privacy policy. Where the law requires it, we'll ask for your consent first, and you'll be able to opt out of personalised ads.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites. Their privacy practices are their own, so check their policies when you visit them.

Children

The site is a general-audience tool. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes too. Questions about privacy? Email us using the address on the contact page.
