Core i5-10400F with RX 7700 XT: Bottleneck & FPS

The Core i5-10400F and RX 7700 XT are well balanced at 1440p: about 73 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 57 FPS. At 1080p the Core i5-10400F becomes the limit (19% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from 1440p upward the RX 7700 XT takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 215 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 48 FPS with a 49% CPU bottleneck. Ray tracing costs this AMD card more than an equivalent NVIDIA GPU: about 31 FPS with RT on at 1440p.

Core i5-10400F + RX 7700 XT: quick answers

Does the Core i5-10400F bottleneck the RX 7700 XT?

1080p: 19% CPU-limited. 1440p: 14% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 28% GPU-limited. 4K: 48% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i5-10400F with RX 7700 XT get?

About 86 FPS at 1080p, 73 FPS at 1440p, 62 FPS at UW 1440p, 46 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 215 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i5-10400F + RX 7700 XT build?

None is needed at 1440p. The Core i5-10400F and RX 7700 XT are well matched, so upgrade only when you want more FPS overall.
