Core Ultra 7 265K with RTX 4070 Ti Super: Bottleneck & FPS

The Core Ultra 7 265K and RTX 4070 Ti Super pair well, with the graphics card setting the pace at 1440p: about 111 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 87 FPS. The RTX 4070 Ti Super is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 20-core Core Ultra 7 265K (LGA1851) still has headroom for about 150 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 341 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 82 FPS with a 41% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 65 FPS at 1440p.

Core Ultra 7 265K + RTX 4070 Ti Super: quick answers

Does the Core Ultra 7 265K bottleneck the RTX 4070 Ti Super?

1080p: balanced. 1440p: 25% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 37% GPU-limited. 4K: 55% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core Ultra 7 265K with RTX 4070 Ti Super get?

About 142 FPS at 1080p, 111 FPS at 1440p, 94 FPS at UW 1440p, 68 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 341 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Core Ultra 7 265K + RTX 4070 Ti Super build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 5080. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
