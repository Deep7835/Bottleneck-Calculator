Ryzen 7 5800X3D with RTX 4070 Ti Super: Bottleneck & FPS

Paired with the RTX 4070 Ti Super, the Ryzen 7 5800X3D delivers roughly 110 FPS at 1440p (1% lows ≈90) in modern AAA games, which our model rates as “well balanced (GPU-bound)”. At 1080p the Ryzen 7 5800X3D becomes the limit (13% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from 1440p upward the RTX 4070 Ti Super takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 332 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 77 FPS with a 45% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 65 FPS at 1440p.

Ryzen 7 5800X3D + RTX 4070 Ti Super: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 7 5800X3D bottleneck the RTX 4070 Ti Super?

1080p: 13% CPU-limited. 1440p: 20% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 33% GPU-limited. 4K: 51% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 7 5800X3D with RTX 4070 Ti Super get?

About 135 FPS at 1080p, 110 FPS at 1440p, 93 FPS at UW 1440p, 68 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 332 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 7 5800X3D + RTX 4070 Ti Super build?

None is needed at 1440p. The Ryzen 7 5800X3D and RTX 4070 Ti Super are well matched, so upgrade only when you want more FPS overall.
