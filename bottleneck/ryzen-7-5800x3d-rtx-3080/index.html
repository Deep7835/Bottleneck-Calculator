Ryzen 7 5800X3D with RTX 3080: Bottleneck & FPS

Paired with the RTX 3080, the Ryzen 7 5800X3D delivers roughly 88 FPS at 1440p (1% lows ≈72) in modern AAA games, which our model rates as “heavily GPU-limited”. The RTX 3080 is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 8-core Ryzen 7 5800X3D (AM4) still has headroom for about 140 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 278 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 76 FPS with a 30% CPU bottleneck. Its 10 GB of VRAM is fine at 1440p but gets tight at 4K High. With ray tracing on, expect about 46 FPS at 1440p.

Ryzen 7 5800X3D + RTX 3080: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 7 5800X3D bottleneck the RTX 3080?

1080p: balanced. 1440p: 37% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 47% GPU-limited. 4K: 64% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 7 5800X3D with RTX 3080 get?

About 121 FPS at 1080p, 88 FPS at 1440p, 74 FPS at UW 1440p, 51 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 278 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 7 5800X3D + RTX 3080 build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 5080 or RTX 4070 Ti Super. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
