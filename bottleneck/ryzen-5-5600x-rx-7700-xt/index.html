Ryzen 5 5600X with RX 7700 XT: Bottleneck & FPS

Paired with the RX 7700 XT, the Ryzen 5 5600X delivers roughly 75 FPS at 1440p (1% lows ≈59) in modern AAA games, which our model rates as “GPU-limited, CPU has headroom”. The RX 7700 XT is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 6-core Ryzen 5 5600X (AM4) still has headroom for about 108 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 234 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 59 FPS with a 37% CPU bottleneck. Ray tracing costs this AMD card more than an equivalent NVIDIA GPU: about 31 FPS with RT on at 1440p.

Ryzen 5 5600X + RX 7700 XT: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 5 5600X bottleneck the RX 7700 XT?

1080p: balanced. 1440p: 30% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 42% GPU-limited. 4K: 58% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 5 5600X with RX 7700 XT get?

About 99 FPS at 1080p, 75 FPS at 1440p, 63 FPS at UW 1440p, 46 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 234 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 5 5600X + RX 7700 XT build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 4080 Super or RX 7800 XT. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
