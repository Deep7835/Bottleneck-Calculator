Core i5-11400F with RTX 5060: Bottleneck & FPS

At 1080p, a Core i5-11400F + RTX 5060 build averages about 86 FPS in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows near 66 FPS: well balanced (GPU-bound). The RTX 5060 is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 6-core Core i5-11400F (LGA1200) still has headroom for about 100 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 248 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 55 FPS with a 51% CPU bottleneck. Its 8 GB of VRAM is fine at 1080p but gets tight at 4K High. With ray tracing on, expect about 48 FPS at 1080p.

Core i5-11400F + RTX 5060: quick answers

Does the Core i5-11400F bottleneck the RTX 5060?

1080p: 10% GPU-limited. 1440p: 42% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 53% GPU-limited. 4K: 68% GPU-limited. At 1080p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i5-11400F with RTX 5060 get?

About 86 FPS at 1080p, 58 FPS at 1440p, 47 FPS at UW 1440p, 32 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 248 FPS at 1080p.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i5-11400F + RTX 5060 build?

None is needed at 1080p. The Core i5-11400F and RTX 5060 are well matched, so upgrade only when you want more FPS overall.
