Ryzen 5 9600X with RTX 5070 Ti: Bottleneck & FPS

The Ryzen 5 9600X and RTX 5070 Ti are well balanced at 1440p: about 120 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 94 FPS. At 1080p the Ryzen 5 9600X becomes the limit (16% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from 1440p upward the RTX 5070 Ti takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 358 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 81 FPS with a 47% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 72 FPS at 1440p.

Ryzen 5 9600X + RTX 5070 Ti: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 5 9600X bottleneck the RTX 5070 Ti?

1080p: 16% CPU-limited. 1440p: 16% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 30% GPU-limited. 4K: 49% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 5 9600X with RTX 5070 Ti get?

About 144 FPS at 1080p, 120 FPS at 1440p, 102 FPS at UW 1440p, 75 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 358 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 5 9600X + RTX 5070 Ti build?

None is needed at 1440p. The Ryzen 5 9600X and RTX 5070 Ti are well matched, so upgrade only when you want more FPS overall.
