Ryzen 7 7800X3D with RX 7900 XT: Bottleneck & FPS

At 1440p, a Ryzen 7 7800X3D + RX 7900 XT build averages about 112 FPS in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows near 92 FPS: heavily GPU-limited. The RX 7900 XT is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 8-core Ryzen 7 7800X3D (AM5) still has headroom for about 176 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 354 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 96 FPS with a 31% CPU bottleneck. Ray tracing costs this AMD card more than an equivalent NVIDIA GPU: about 46 FPS with RT on at 1440p.

Ryzen 7 7800X3D + RX 7900 XT: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 7 7800X3D bottleneck the RX 7900 XT?

1080p: balanced. 1440p: 36% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 47% GPU-limited. 4K: 61% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 7 7800X3D with RX 7900 XT get?

About 153 FPS at 1080p, 112 FPS at 1440p, 94 FPS at UW 1440p, 68 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 354 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 7 7800X3D + RX 7900 XT build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 5090 or RTX 5080. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
