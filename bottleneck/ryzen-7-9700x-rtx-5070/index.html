Ryzen 7 9700X with RTX 5070: Bottleneck & FPS

At 1440p, a Ryzen 7 9700X + RTX 5070 build averages about 101 FPS in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows near 79 FPS: GPU-limited, CPU has headroom. The RTX 5070 is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 8-core Ryzen 7 9700X (AM5) still has headroom for about 154 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 317 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 84 FPS with a 33% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 59 FPS at 1440p, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Ryzen 7 9700X + RTX 5070: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 7 9700X bottleneck the RTX 5070?

1080p: balanced. 1440p: 34% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 45% GPU-limited. 4K: 60% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 7 9700X with RTX 5070 get?

About 136 FPS at 1080p, 101 FPS at 1440p, 84 FPS at UW 1440p, 61 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 317 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 7 9700X + RTX 5070 build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 5090 or RTX 5070 Ti. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
