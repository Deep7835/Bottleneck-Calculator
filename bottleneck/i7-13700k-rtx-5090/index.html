Core i7-13700K with RTX 5090: Bottleneck & FPS

At 4K, a Core i7-13700K + RTX 5090 build averages about 125 FPS in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows near 98 FPS: GPU-limited, CPU has headroom. At 1080p and 1440p the Core i7-13700K becomes the limit (47% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from UW 1440p upward the RTX 5090 takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 378 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 88 FPS with a 45% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 74 FPS at 4K.

Core i7-13700K + RTX 5090: quick answers

Does the Core i7-13700K bottleneck the RTX 5090?

1080p: 47% CPU-limited. 1440p: 24% CPU-limited. UW 1440p: balanced. 4K: 20% GPU-limited. At 4K the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i7-13700K with RTX 5090 get?

About 160 FPS at 1080p, 158 FPS at 1440p, 152 FPS at UW 1440p, 125 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 378 FPS at 4K.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i7-13700K + RTX 5090 build?

There is no faster graphics card to move to, and the Core i7-13700K already keeps the RTX 5090 fed at 4K. Use DLSS or FSR upscaling for more FPS.
