Core i5-12400F with RTX 3060 12GB: Bottleneck & FPS

The Core i5-12400F and RTX 3060 12GB leave the CPU with plenty of spare headroom at 1080p: about 64 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 50 FPS. The RTX 3060 12GB is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 6-core Core i5-12400F (LGA1700) still has headroom for about 120 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 205 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 64 FPS with a 18% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 35 FPS at 1080p, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Core i5-12400F + RTX 3060 12GB: quick answers

Does the Core i5-12400F bottleneck the RTX 3060 12GB?

1080p: 46% GPU-limited. 1440p: 62% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 69% GPU-limited. 4K: 77% GPU-limited. At 1080p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i5-12400F with RTX 3060 12GB get?

About 64 FPS at 1080p, 45 FPS at 1440p, 38 FPS at UW 1440p, 27 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 205 FPS at 1080p.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i5-12400F + RTX 3060 12GB build?

At 1080p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 4060 Ti 8GB or RTX 4070 Super. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
