Ryzen 5 5600 with RX 9070 XT: Bottleneck & FPS

The Ryzen 5 5600 and RX 9070 XT are well matched, with only a minor CPU bottleneck at 1440p: about 102 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 73 FPS. At 1080p and 1440p the Ryzen 5 5600 becomes the limit (39% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from UW 1440p upward the RX 9070 XT takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 274 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 58 FPS with a 62% CPU bottleneck. Ray tracing costs this AMD card more than an equivalent NVIDIA GPU: about 60 FPS with RT on at 1440p.

Ryzen 5 5600 + RX 9070 XT: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 5 5600 bottleneck the RX 9070 XT?

1080p: 39% CPU-limited. 1440p: 13% CPU-limited. UW 1440p: balanced. 4K: 30% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 5 5600 with RX 9070 XT get?

About 106 FPS at 1080p, 102 FPS at 1440p, 95 FPS at UW 1440p, 73 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 274 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 5 5600 + RX 9070 XT build?

None is needed at 1440p. The Ryzen 5 5600 and RX 9070 XT are well matched, so upgrade only when you want more FPS overall.
