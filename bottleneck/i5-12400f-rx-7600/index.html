Core i5-12400F with RX 7600: Bottleneck & FPS

At 1080p, a Core i5-12400F + RX 7600 build averages about 73 FPS in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows near 57 FPS: heavily GPU-limited. The RX 7600 is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 6-core Core i5-12400F (LGA1700) still has headroom for about 120 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 233 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 65 FPS with a 28% CPU bottleneck. Its 8 GB of VRAM is fine at 1080p but gets tight at 4K High. With ray tracing on, expect about 26 FPS at 1080p.

Core i5-12400F + RX 7600: quick answers

Does the Core i5-12400F bottleneck the RX 7600?

1080p: 39% GPU-limited. 1440p: 60% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 68% GPU-limited. 4K: 78% GPU-limited. At 1080p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i5-12400F with RX 7600 get?

About 73 FPS at 1080p, 48 FPS at 1440p, 39 FPS at UW 1440p, 26 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 233 FPS at 1080p.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i5-12400F + RX 7600 build?

At 1080p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 5060 Ti 8GB or RX 9060 XT 8GB. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
