Ryzen 7 7700X with RX 6800 XT: Bottleneck & FPS

Paired with the RX 6800 XT, the Ryzen 7 7700X delivers roughly 87 FPS at 1440p (1% lows ≈68) in modern AAA games, which our model rates as “heavily GPU-limited”. The RX 6800 XT is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 8-core Ryzen 7 7700X (AM5) still has headroom for about 144 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 275 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 78 FPS with a 27% CPU bottleneck. Ray tracing costs this AMD card more than an equivalent NVIDIA GPU: about 28 FPS with RT on at 1440p.

Ryzen 7 7700X + RX 6800 XT: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 7 7700X bottleneck the RX 6800 XT?

1080p: 14% GPU-limited. 1440p: 40% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 50% GPU-limited. 4K: 64% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 7 7700X with RX 6800 XT get?

About 120 FPS at 1080p, 87 FPS at 1440p, 72 FPS at UW 1440p, 53 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 275 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 7 7700X + RX 6800 XT build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 4080 Super or RX 9070 XT. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
