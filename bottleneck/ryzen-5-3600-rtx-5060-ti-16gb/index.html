Ryzen 5 3600 with RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: Bottleneck & FPS

Paired with the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB, the Ryzen 5 3600 delivers roughly 72 FPS at 1440p (1% lows ≈54) in modern AAA games, which our model rates as “excellent balance”. At 1080p the Ryzen 5 3600 becomes the limit (28% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from 1440p upward the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 202 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 44 FPS with a 54% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 45 FPS at 1440p, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Ryzen 5 3600 + RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 5 3600 bottleneck the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB?

1080p: 28% CPU-limited. 1440p: balanced. UW 1440p: 20% GPU-limited. 4K: 42% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 5 3600 with RTX 5060 Ti 16GB get?

About 79 FPS at 1080p, 72 FPS at 1440p, 63 FPS at UW 1440p, 47 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 202 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 5 3600 + RTX 5060 Ti 16GB build?

None is needed at 1440p. The Ryzen 5 3600 and RTX 5060 Ti 16GB are well matched, so upgrade only when you want more FPS overall.
