Core i5-13600K with RTX 4090: Bottleneck & FPS

Paired with the RTX 4090, the Core i5-13600K delivers roughly 97 FPS at 4K (1% lows ≈76) in modern AAA games, which our model rates as “heavily GPU-limited”. At 1080p the Core i5-13600K becomes the limit (35% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from 1440p upward the RTX 4090 takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 305 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 82 FPS with a 32% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 56 FPS at 4K, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Core i5-13600K + RTX 4090: quick answers

Does the Core i5-13600K bottleneck the RTX 4090?

1080p: 35% CPU-limited. 1440p: balanced. UW 1440p: 11% GPU-limited. 4K: 35% GPU-limited. At 4K the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i5-13600K with RTX 4090 get?

About 149 FPS at 1080p, 142 FPS at 1440p, 128 FPS at UW 1440p, 97 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 305 FPS at 4K.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i5-13600K + RTX 4090 build?

At 4K, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 5090. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
