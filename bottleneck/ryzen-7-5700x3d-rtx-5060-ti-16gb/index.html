Ryzen 7 5700X3D with RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: Bottleneck & FPS

The Ryzen 7 5700X3D and RTX 5060 Ti 16GB leave the CPU with plenty of spare headroom at 1440p: about 77 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 63 FPS. The RTX 5060 Ti 16GB is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 8-core Ryzen 7 5700X3D (AM4) still has headroom for about 132 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 245 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 72 FPS with a 25% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 45 FPS at 1440p, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Ryzen 7 5700X3D + RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 7 5700X3D bottleneck the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB?

1080p: 16% GPU-limited. 1440p: 42% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 51% GPU-limited. 4K: 65% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 7 5700X3D with RTX 5060 Ti 16GB get?

About 107 FPS at 1080p, 77 FPS at 1440p, 64 FPS at UW 1440p, 47 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 245 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 7 5700X3D + RTX 5060 Ti 16GB build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 4070 Ti Super or RX 9070. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
