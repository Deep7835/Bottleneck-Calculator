Ryzen 7 9700X with RTX 5080: Bottleneck & FPS

The Ryzen 7 9700X and RTX 5080 leave the CPU with plenty of spare headroom at 4K: about 86 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 67 FPS. At 1080p the Ryzen 7 9700X becomes the limit (24% CPU bottleneck at 1080p); from 1440p upward the RTX 5080 takes over and the CPU has capacity to spare. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 272 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 83 FPS with a 21% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 50 FPS at 4K, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Ryzen 7 9700X + RTX 5080: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 7 9700X bottleneck the RTX 5080?

1080p: 24% CPU-limited. 1440p: balanced. UW 1440p: 23% GPU-limited. 4K: 44% GPU-limited. At 4K the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 7 9700X with RTX 5080 get?

About 152 FPS at 1080p, 134 FPS at 1440p, 116 FPS at UW 1440p, 86 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 272 FPS at 4K.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 7 9700X + RTX 5080 build?

No graphics card is a big enough step up from the RTX 5080 to be worth it with the Ryzen 7 9700X at 4K. Use DLSS or FSR upscaling for more FPS.
