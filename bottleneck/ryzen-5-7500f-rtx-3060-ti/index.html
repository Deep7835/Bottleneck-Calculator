Ryzen 5 7500F with RTX 3060 Ti: Bottleneck & FPS

Paired with the RTX 3060 Ti, the Ryzen 5 7500F delivers roughly 83 FPS at 1080p (1% lows ≈64) in modern AAA games, which our model rates as “heavily GPU-limited”. The RTX 3060 Ti is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 6-core Ryzen 5 7500F (AM5) still has headroom for about 134 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 261 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 73 FPS with a 29% CPU bottleneck. Its 8 GB of VRAM is fine at 1080p but gets tight at 4K High. With ray tracing on, expect about 42 FPS at 1080p.

Ryzen 5 7500F + RTX 3060 Ti: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 5 7500F bottleneck the RTX 3060 Ti?

1080p: 38% GPU-limited. 1440p: 60% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 68% GPU-limited. 4K: 78% GPU-limited. At 1080p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 5 7500F with RTX 3060 Ti get?

About 83 FPS at 1080p, 54 FPS at 1440p, 43 FPS at UW 1440p, 30 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 261 FPS at 1080p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 5 7500F + RTX 3060 Ti build?

At 1080p, the most effective upgrade is the RX 7800 XT or RTX 5060 Ti 8GB. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
