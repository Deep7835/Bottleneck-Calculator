Core i5-12600K with RX 6700 XT: Bottleneck & FPS

The Core i5-12600K and RX 6700 XT leave the CPU with plenty of spare headroom at 1440p: about 58 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 45 FPS. The RX 6700 XT is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 10-core Core i5-12600K (LGA1700) still has headroom for about 136 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 185 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 67 FPS. Ray tracing costs this AMD card more than an equivalent NVIDIA GPU: about 18 FPS with RT on at 1440p.

Core i5-12600K + RX 6700 XT: quick answers

Does the Core i5-12600K bottleneck the RX 6700 XT?

1080p: 39% GPU-limited. 1440p: 57% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 65% GPU-limited. 4K: 74% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i5-12600K with RX 6700 XT get?

About 83 FPS at 1080p, 58 FPS at 1440p, 48 FPS at UW 1440p, 35 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 185 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i5-12600K + RX 6700 XT build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 4070 or RX 9070. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
