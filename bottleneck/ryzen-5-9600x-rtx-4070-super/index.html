Ryzen 5 9600X with RTX 4070 Super: Bottleneck & FPS

At 1440p, a Ryzen 5 9600X + RTX 4070 Super build averages about 96 FPS in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows near 75 FPS: GPU-limited, CPU has headroom. The RTX 4070 Super is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 6-core Ryzen 5 9600X (AM5) still has headroom for about 148 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 303 FPS, enough for a 240 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 81 FPS with a 32% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 56 FPS at 1440p, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Ryzen 5 9600X + RTX 4070 Super: quick answers

Does the Ryzen 5 9600X bottleneck the RTX 4070 Super?

1080p: balanced. 1440p: 35% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 46% GPU-limited. 4K: 61% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Ryzen 5 9600X with RTX 4070 Super get?

About 130 FPS at 1080p, 96 FPS at 1440p, 80 FPS at UW 1440p, 58 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 303 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Ryzen 5 9600X + RTX 4070 Super build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RTX 5090 or RX 9070 XT. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
