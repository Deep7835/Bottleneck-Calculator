Core i5-12400F with RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: Bottleneck & FPS

The Core i5-12400F and RTX 5060 Ti 16GB leave the CPU with plenty of spare headroom at 1440p: about 77 FPS on average in AAA games at High settings, with 1% lows around 60 FPS. The RTX 5060 Ti 16GB is the limiting part at every resolution, so the 6-core Core i5-12400F (LGA1700) still has headroom for about 120 FPS and room for a faster card later. In esports titles such as CS2 or Valorant expect around 242 FPS, enough for a 144 Hz monitor, while CPU-heavy games like Microsoft Flight Simulator drop to about 66 FPS with a 32% CPU bottleneck. With ray tracing enabled, expect about 45 FPS at 1440p, so turn on DLSS or FSR to get back above 60.

Core i5-12400F + RTX 5060 Ti 16GB: quick answers

Does the Core i5-12400F bottleneck the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB?

1080p: balanced. 1440p: 36% GPU-limited. UW 1440p: 46% GPU-limited. 4K: 61% GPU-limited. At 1440p the CPU keeps up, so this is a sensible pairing. A GPU-limited result is the normal, healthy state for gaming.

How many FPS does the Core i5-12400F with RTX 5060 Ti 16GB get?

About 105 FPS at 1080p, 77 FPS at 1440p, 64 FPS at UW 1440p, 47 FPS at 4K in AAA games at High settings without upscaling. In esports games like CS2 and Valorant it reaches roughly 242 FPS at 1440p.

What is the best upgrade for a Core i5-12400F + RTX 5060 Ti 16GB build?

At 1440p, the most effective upgrade is the RX 9070 XT or RTX 4070 Super. Use the calculator to check other resolutions and settings.
